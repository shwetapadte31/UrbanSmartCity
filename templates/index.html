ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Turning civic problems into urban intelligence.

Urban Smart City is an AI-powered civic intelligence platform that transforms citizen-reported problems into structured, analyzed and actionable information for better municipal monitoring and decision-making.

01

Citizen Reporting

Citizens can report potholes, garbage, water leakage, drainage, streetlight issues, road damage and other civic problems with location and images.

02

AI Analysis

The system analyzes reported problems and helps identify their type, severity, priority and responsible department.

03

Municipal Monitoring

Municipal officers can monitor complaints, manage their status and view important city intelligence.

04

Urban Intelligence

Historical complaint data supports heatmaps, trends, recurring-problem detection, forecasting and risk analysis.

HOW IT WORKS

From citizen report to civic intelligence.

The platform connects citizen reporting, AI analysis, municipal action and citizen verification into one integrated workflow.

01

Report

A citizen submits a civic problem with a description, location and optional image.

02

Analyze

AI analyzes the complaint and identifies its type, severity, priority and responsible department.

03

Assign & Monitor

Municipal officers manage complaints and monitor their progress through the resolution lifecycle.

04

Resolve

Officers update the complaint and submit the resolution when the civic problem has been addressed.

05

Verify & Learn

Citizens verify the resolution while historical data contributes to wider urban intelligence.
